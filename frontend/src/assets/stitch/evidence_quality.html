Human-in-the-Loop Supervisory Qualification

Evidence quality indicators describe empirical usability of submitted records. They do not independently determine statutory non-compliance. The appointed supervisory examiner must review, qualify, or issue statutory Evidence Injunctions prior to regulatory sanction.
